About MORPHEUS

South City International School, situated in the cultural heart of Kolkata, continues its proud legacy as a centre of academic excellence and holistic development. With an enduring commitment to nurturing creativity, curiosity and character, the institution encourages young learners to explore the world with imagination, confidence and purpose.

The school proudly presents the 8th edition of Morpheus, themed “Ascension of Aspirations.” Inspired by the spirit of transcending limitations, Morpheus 2026 invites students to venture beyond the familiar and enter a realm where bold ideas, artistic brilliance and innovative thought converge.

Bringing together participants from diverse schools and creative disciplines, the fest offers a dynamic platform where performance meets passion, intellect meets imagination and collaboration sparks new possibilities. Through an exciting range of competitions, showcases and interactive experiences, Morpheus inspires students to express themselves, challenge conventions and redefine what they believe is possible.

As we celebrate talent, ambition and the transformative power of youthful vision, we invite you to join us on this remarkable journey.

Welcome to Morpheus 2026—where aspirations ascend and dreams gain the power to reshape the world.
